FAQ
Questions, answered.
Is this just ChatGPT with my data pasted in?

No. Nixara reads your role along with your question and recalibrates the same dataset into a different report for each seat — a generic LLM chat gives everyone the same answer and forgets it the moment the thread ends.

Do I need an account?

No. Three reports are free per session with no signup. Paste your own API key later for unlimited access — it never leaves your browser.

What data sources are supported?

CSV, Excel, or a live Tableau connection today. Nixara reads the underlying data directly rather than a copy-pasted snippet.

How does outcome tracking work?

Every recommendation gets a decision id. Log what actually happened later, and Nixara compares it against the original prediction to build an accuracy record.
