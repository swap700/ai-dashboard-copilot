"Isn't this just pasting my spreadsheet into an LLM?"
Structurally, no. A chat window holds one thread, one voice, and forgets everything the moment you close the tab. Nixara reads your file directly — no manual copy-paste, no truncation — routes it through six distinct role lenses instead of one generic answer, and logs every recommendation so it can be checked later against what actually happened. That last part is the piece a generic assistant has no way to do: it doesn't know if it was right, because nobody ever tells it.
