Issue 01 — decision intelligence, personal and tracked
Nixara is not a dashboard. It is the fifteen minutes a founder used to lose every week, rebuilding the same slide from a different angle for a different person in the room.

A CFO and a CEO can look at identical numbers and need entirely different sentences back. One wants the risk named first. The other wants the opportunity named first. Most software gives both of them the same chart.

Nixara reads the role before it reads the request. Upload a spreadsheet, ask a plain question, and the report that comes back is written for the seat you sit in — not a generic export with your name in the header.

Every recommendation becomes a logged decision: approved, rejected, or postponed, with an id and a timestamp. Months later, Nixara checks its own homework — comparing what it predicted against what actually happened.

That loop is the part most tools skip entirely. A forecast that is never checked against reality is just a guess with better formatting. Nixara's is starting to build a track record instead.

None of this is what happens when you paste a spreadsheet into a chat window. A generic assistant gives whoever asks the same paragraph, forgets it the moment the thread closes, and has no way of knowing later whether it was right. Nixara reads the seat along with the question, keeps a record of every call, and checks itself against what actually happened — the difference between an answer and an accountable one.
